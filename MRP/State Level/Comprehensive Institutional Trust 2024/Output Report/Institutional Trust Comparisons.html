2024 Trust Across Institutions

Overview

Overall, schools are fairly middle of the row in institutional trust, with a mean of 0.354. However, so far as I can tell from this data, trust in them does appear to function differently.

As an institution, schools are highly individually highly partisan, with a strong Democratic lean (PID coefficient -1.41) like universities and media. However, it’s also weakly predicted by state partisanship (trump_z coefficient close to 0, and the correlation with trump_z is only r = -0.58). Furthermore, schools had the second highest state-level variance unexplained by the model (behind only religious leaders, with religion not being regressed on).

With regards to demographics, schools had a much flatter age gradient than most other institutions. The other main finding was a large racial category gap, which was significantly larger for public schools than universities.

Unexplained Variance

Schools have notably higher variation unexplained model at the state-level than other institutions (second only to religious leaders). Most institutions clustered between 0.06 and 0.1 residual state SD. Only religious leaders higher than schools (perhaps because not MRPing on religion). Partisanship explains most geographic variation for universities/media but not for schools.

Limitation

Parental status not included (though interestingly age range is flat). Also no deep MRP interaction for (pid3:trump_z), though given the lack of contextual effects shouldn’t matter too much.
